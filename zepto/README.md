# ZEPTO: Sales Performance & Growth Opportunity Analysis

## Background and Overview
Zepto is a grocery delivery platform. 

This analysis was conducted to provide senior leadership with a clear overview of sales performance from the company's inception through the most recent data. The primary goals are to identify key revenue drivers, uncover performance trends over time, and provide actionable recommendations to guide strategic decisions.

## Data Overview

The analysis is based on a dataset of 3000+records. The data includes the following primary table:

Zepto: Contains data, including category, product name,	mrp,discountPercent, available quantity,	discounted selling price, weight in grams, products out of stock, and quantity.

This dataset provides a detailed view of the product catalog and pricing structure of Zepto, a fast-growing 10-minute grocery delivery platform. The data captures essential attributes for over 3,000+ SKUs (Stock Keeping Units) across various categories like Fruits & Vegetables, Dairy, Packaged Foods, Beverages, and more.
