# MavenFlix: Subscriber Retention & Revenue Analysis (Sept 2022 - Sept 2023)

## Background and Overview

MavenFlix is a fictitious video streaming platform. This analysis was conducted to provide a clear overview of subscriber performance from September 2022 to September 2023.

The primary goals are to identify key subscription trends, understand customer retention and churn, and analyze core financial metrics (MRR, ARR, ARPU, and LTV). The insights from this report are intended to guide strategic decisions in finance and platform development to improve subscriber growth and profitability.

## Data Overview

The analysis is based on a dataset of approximately 2,900 subscriber records from September 2022 through September 2023.

The dataset (Subscription Cohort Analysis Data.csv) contains subscription-level data, including customer ID, subscription created and canceled dates, cost, interval (monthly/annual), and payment status.
