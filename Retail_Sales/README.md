
# : Sales Performance & Growth Opportunity Analysis

## Background and Overview
"Company Name" is a UK based online retailer.

This analysis was conducted to provide senior leadership with a clear overview of sales performance from the company's inception through the most recent data. The primary goals are to identify key revenue drivers, uncover performance trends over time, and provide actionable recommendations to guide strategic decisions in marketing, product management, and platform development.

## Data Overview
The analysis is based on a dataset of 50,000+ transactions occurring between 01/12/2010 and 09/12/2011. The data includes the following primary tables

  **Transactions:** Contains transactional data, including invoice number, stock code, description, quantity, invoice date, unit price, customerid, and country.


## Executive Summary
